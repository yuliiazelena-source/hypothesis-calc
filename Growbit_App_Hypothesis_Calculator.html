How to use this calculator

The idea

A hypothesis says "this lifts trial-start by 8%". Enter it, see what it does to revenue and ARPU, save it, and compare hypotheses to find the highest-leverage bets.

Model

Revenue = Installs × Conversion × ARPPU. Purchases split into two paths: Weekly purchases = Install → Trial → Purchase. Annual purchases = direct purchases without trial. Lifting a step rate grows the trial path; annual purchases are held constant.

Goal types

% uplift ARPU is recommended — ARPU is per-install, so seasonal traffic swings don't move the goal. Absolute $ targets total revenue.

Saving & exporting

Save several hypotheses, tick the ones in plan, and the bars show compounded progress toward the goal. Export to Excel/CSV to share.
⚠ ARPPU 1y is below ARPPU 6m. 1y is cumulative, so it should be ≥ 6m.
